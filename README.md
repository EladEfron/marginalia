# Marginalia

Handwritten study notes for iPad. Runs as a home-screen app: full screen, its own icon, works offline.

## Install on iPad
1. Open the site's GitHub Pages address in Safari.
2. Tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
3. Open Marginalia from the new icon.

## Features
- Apple Pencil ink with pressure (fountain or ballpoint), highlighter that sits behind ink, whole-stroke or precise eraser
- Palm rejection: when a Pencil is detected, fingers scroll and zoom; two-finger tap undoes, three-finger tap redoes
- Hold at the end of a stroke to snap it into a line, circle, ellipse, rectangle or triangle
- Lasso to move, resize, recolour, duplicate or delete
- Typed text boxes, searchable from the library
- Images
- Import PDFs (lecture slides) and write on them
- Paper: blank, ruled, narrow ruled, grid, dotted, Cornell; white, cream or dark
- Lecture recording synced to your notes: play back and tap any line of ink to hear that moment; "Ink replay" fades notes written later
- Pages panel with reorder, duplicate, delete and bookmarks
- Notebooks grouped by subject with coloured covers
- Export notebook as PDF or a page as PNG through the iPad share sheet
- Backup and restore all notebooks as one file

Notes are stored on the device (IndexedDB). Use **Backup** regularly to keep a copy in Files or iCloud Drive.
